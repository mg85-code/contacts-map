📲 Get the app on your phone

On Android (in Chrome):
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top-right of Chrome.
  2. Tap “Install app” (on some phones it says “Add to Home screen”).
  3. Tap Install — the app icon appears on your home screen.
On iPhone (in Safari):
  1. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  2. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. Tap Add — the app icon appears on your home screen.
